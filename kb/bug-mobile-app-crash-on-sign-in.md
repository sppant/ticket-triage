# The mobile app crashes when signing in

If the mobile app closes immediately after you tap Sign in, update to the latest version first; a crash on sign-in caused by an expired session token was fixed in the most recent release.

If the crash continues, clear the app cache (Settings, Apps, Storage, Clear cache) and sign in again. As a last step, uninstall and reinstall the app; your data is stored on our servers and is not lost.

You can keep working in the browser meanwhile. When you report the crash, include your phone model, operating system version and app version. Crashes that block a whole team are handled as high priority.
