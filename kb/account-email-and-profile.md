# Changing your email address and display name

To change your display name, open Settings, Profile and edit Display name; the new name applies immediately everywhere, including your middle initial if you add one.

To change the email address on your account, open Settings, Profile, Email and enter the new address. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect once you click it. Your history, workspace memberships and billing stay attached to the account.

If you can no longer sign in with the old address, contact support from the old address if possible; we verify ownership before moving the account.
