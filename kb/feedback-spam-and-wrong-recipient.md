# Feedback, spam and messages sent by mistake

Thank you for kind words; we pass compliments on to the support team. Feedback about the product is welcome at any time through the feedback board.

If you received a message from us that you did not expect, it was most likely sent to the wrong address by mistake. Use the unsubscribe link in the message, and we will remove the address from our lists.

We never ask you to click a link to claim a prize. Messages like that are spam; report them to support and delete them without clicking.
