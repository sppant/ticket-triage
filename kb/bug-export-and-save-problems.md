# Exports with missing rows and edits that do not save

CSV and PDF exports include every row that matches the active filters. If the last row is missing, check that no filter or grouping is hiding it, and re-run the export from the full list view. Known issue: exports of very large reports can drop the final row; re-exporting in smaller date ranges is the workaround until the fix ships.

If edits disappear after you click Save, check your connection; the page shows a spinner when the save request fails to complete. Copy your text before retrying. If the problem repeats, try a private browser window to rule out an extension, and send us the time it happened. Lost work is treated as high priority and we can often restore it from version history.
