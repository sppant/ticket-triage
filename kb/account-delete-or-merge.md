# Deleting or merging accounts

To delete your account, open Settings, Privacy, Delete account. Deletion removes your profile and personal data within 30 days; shared workspace content stays with the workspace owner. Export your data first, because deletion cannot be undone.

Two accounts with different email addresses cannot be merged automatically. Support can move the workspaces and content of one account into the other: contact us from both addresses and tell us which account to keep. The account that is not kept is closed afterwards.
