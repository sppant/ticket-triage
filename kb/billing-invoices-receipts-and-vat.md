# Invoices, receipts, VAT numbers and currency

Invoices and receipts are available under Settings, Billing, Invoice history, as PDF files for every payment. We can also email a receipt to any address on request.

To add a VAT or tax number, enter it under Billing details. It appears on every invoice issued after the change; past invoices can be reissued on request, free of charge.

The invoice currency is fixed when the subscription is created. If an invoice was issued in the wrong currency, contact billing with the contract reference. We cancel the invoice and reissue it in the contracted currency, usually within 2 business days.
