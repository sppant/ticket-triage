# Single sign-on and audit logs

Single sign-on with SAML and an audit log of admin actions are enterprise features. SAML SSO is available on the enterprise plan today; setup requires your identity provider metadata and takes about a day with our help.

The audit log records sign-ins, role changes and settings changes, and can be exported as CSV. If you are on another plan and need either feature for a compliance review or a renewal deadline, contact sales; we can enable a trial for the duration of your review.
