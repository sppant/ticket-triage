# Changing seats, plans and annual discounts

You can add or remove seats at any time under Settings, Billing, Seats. Added seats are charged pro rata immediately. Removed seats take effect at the next billing cycle, and the credit for unused seats is applied to your next invoice, so the current invoice may still show the old amount.

Switching from monthly to annual billing gives a discount of two free months per year. Annual plans are invoiced once, up front, and can be switched back to monthly at renewal.

Plan changes between tiers are pro rated. For volume pricing above 50 seats, contact sales.
