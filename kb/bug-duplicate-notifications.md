# Duplicate email notifications

Receiving each email notification twice usually means the same address is subscribed through two routes, for example a personal setting and a team rule. Open Settings, Notifications and turn off one of the two.

If you have only one rule and still get duplicates, the cause may be a mail forwarding rule on your side. Check for a filter that forwards to the same inbox.

If neither applies, send us two example emails with their full headers. Duplicate notifications are a medium-priority bug and we usually respond within 2 business days.
