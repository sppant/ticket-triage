# If you think your account was hacked

Signs of a compromised account: a password change you did not make, a login alert from an unfamiliar country, or unknown devices in your session list.

Act immediately: reset your password with Forgot password, then open Settings, Security and choose Sign out of all devices. Turn on two-factor authentication.

Contact support right away. We will lock the account, review the audit trail for unauthorized changes and restore anything that was altered. Security incidents are always handled as high priority.
