# Duplicate charges and failed payments

If your card was charged twice for one subscription period, one of the charges is almost always a duplicate authorization that your bank has not yet released. Check your bank statement: a pending duplicate disappears within 3 to 5 business days.

If both charges have settled, reply to your billing email with the last four digits of the card and the dates. We refund the duplicate in full within 5 business days, back to the original card.

If a payment failed in the app but your bank still shows a charge, the charge is a temporary hold. It is released automatically within 7 days. You can retry the payment right away with the same or a different card; the hold does not count against the new payment.
