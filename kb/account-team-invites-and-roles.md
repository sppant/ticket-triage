# Inviting teammates and workspace roles

Only workspace admins can invite people. If the Invite button is greyed out, you are not an admin, or your plan has no free seats left. Ask an existing admin to promote you under Settings, Members, or add a seat under Billing.

Invitations expire after 7 days and can be resent. Roles are Admin, Member and Viewer; Viewers do not use a paid seat.

An invitation is tied to an email address, so the invitee must sign up with exactly that address.
