# Locked out and two-factor codes

After 5 wrong passwords an account is locked for 30 minutes. Use Forgot password on the sign-in page to reset it immediately; the reset link is valid for 1 hour and also lifts the lock.

If the SMS code for two-factor sign-in does not arrive, wait 60 seconds and request a new one, check that your number is correct and that your phone has signal, and make sure messages from short codes are not blocked. You can also use a recovery code or an authenticator app if you set one up.

If you can no longer use your second factor, contact support from the email address on the account; we verify your identity before disabling two-factor.
