# Visual glitches: typos, dark mode contrast and tooltips

Visual problems such as misspelled labels, low-contrast text in dark mode or flickering tooltips do not affect your data. They are tracked as low-priority bugs and fixed in the regular weekly release.

To help us reproduce a display problem, tell us the browser and version, whether dark mode is on, and the page where you saw it; a screenshot is ideal.

As a workaround for dark mode contrast, switch to the light theme under Settings, Appearance. Tooltip flicker usually stops after a hard refresh of the page.
