# Charges you do not recognise

If you see a charge you did not authorize, contact billing right away and include the date, the amount and the last four digits of the card. We treat unrecognised charges as high priority and respond within 4 business hours.

Before contacting your bank, check whether another admin on your workspace upgraded the plan or added seats; the billing owner receives a receipt for every change. If nobody on your team made the change, we reverse the charge, review the account for unauthorized access and reset the payment method.

Opening a dispute with your bank is your right, but a dispute freezes the account until it is resolved, so write to us first.
