# Offices, press and partnership enquiries

We are a remote-first company and do not have public offices open to visitors. For a meeting while you travel, email us and we will try to arrange a video call or a coffee with a local team member.

Press enquiries go to the communications team at press@example.com. Please include your outlet and deadline.

Our partner program covers resellers, consultancies and agencies. Details and the application form are on the partners page; or write to partners@example.com with a short description of your business. We reply within 5 business days.
